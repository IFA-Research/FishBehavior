Fish Behavior Classification

Online Hunger-Level Recognition of Tilapia from Underwater Video with a MobileViT-Small–BiLSTM Model

Fish Behavior Classification recognizes tilapia hunger level from underwater video in real time, so feed can follow the actual hunger level. A single frame is not enough, because hunger is expressed by how the fish behave over a short time window. MobileViT-Small extracts the spatial appearance and a bidirectional LSTM models the temporal order. The network runs online on an NVIDIA Jetson and reaches accuracy close to models with about three times as many parameters. Recordings were collected with two side-view cameras: Tilapia-ZED (2.3 MP, 19 days) and Tilapia-FD (4 MP, 8 days).
